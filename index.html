01 / The problem

The strategy is sound.
The handoff breaks it.

A growth goal passes through finance, sales, marketing, and customer teams. Each group rebuilds its part in a different spreadsheet. By the time the plan reaches the people delivering it, the assumptions no longer connect.

its working.

Measured

Deterministic

The AI never does the arithmetic.

AI helps guide and explain. A deterministic engine calculates the plan, so the same inputs return the same result.

Honest

Owned

When ambition and capacity disagree, the difference stays on screen until someone resolves it.
