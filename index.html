04 / Built for RevOps

A revenue plan should be
operable, not ornamental.

Traceable

Every assumption keeps its source.

Distinguish measured operating data from team judgment and temporary planning defaults. Review where an input came from before it drives a commitment.

Consistent

One assumption means the same thing everywhere.

Confirmed definitions carry through every view of the plan, so teams can review the result without debating which version or formula produced it.

Reconciled

Top-down ambition meets bottom-up capacity.

Keep the revenue requirement and the operating plan in the same view. When they disagree, the gap remains explicit instead of disappearing into a spreadsheet adjustment.

Accountable

Assumptions become owned decisions.

Give RevOps a reviewable record of what was confirmed, what still needs evidence, and where leadership intervention is required.
